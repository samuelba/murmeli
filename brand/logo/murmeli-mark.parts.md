# Murmeli mark: SVG parts and animation

This note describes `murmeli-mark-animated.svg` so it can be ported to the Angular component `<mu-logo [animated]>`. `murmeli-mark.svg` has the same structure without the `<style>`, the `mu-logo-wink` path and the `mu-logo-sparkle` group.

Do not edit the SVG files by hand. They are generated by `brand/scripts/lib/logo.mjs`, so make changes there and run `node brand/scripts/build.mjs`.

## Structure

Root: `<svg viewBox="0 0 128 128" class="mu-logo mu-logo--animated" role="img" aria-label="Murmeli">`

```
svg.mu-logo[.mu-logo--animated]
├── style                              (only in the animated file)
├── defs
│   ├── linearGradient#mu-logo-sky     sky, top to bottom
│   ├── linearGradient#mu-logo-meadow  hill, top to bottom
│   ├── linearGradient#mu-logo-fur     marmot fur, top to bottom
│   ├── clipPath#mu-logo-tile-clip     rounded tile (x8 y8 112×112, rx30)
│   └── clipPath#mu-logo-burrow-clip   everything above y=93, plus the lower half of the hole
├── rect.mu-logo-shadow                soft drop shadow, tile shifted down by 4
└── g.mu-logo-tile                     (clip-path: tile)
    ├── rect                           sky
    ├── g.mu-logo-mountains            two peaks with snow caps
    ├── g.mu-logo-hill                 meadow and grass tufts
    ├── g.mu-logo-signpost             yellow Swiss hiking signpost
    ├── g.mu-logo-burrow               dirt rim and dark hole (behind the marmot)
    ├── g (clip-path: burrow)          static wrapper; the clip must not move
    │   └── g.mu-logo-marmot           ANIMATED: pop up, squash and stretch, duck
    │       ├── body, belly, ears, head, dark crown, muzzle, cheeks
    │       ├── g.mu-logo-eye.mu-logo-eye-left   ANIMATED: blink
    │       ├── g.mu-logo-eye.mu-logo-eye-right  ANIMATED: blink, then hidden while winking
    │       ├── path.mu-logo-wink                ANIMATED: "^" closed eye, visible only while winking
    │       ├── nose, mouth, teeth, left paw on the rim
    │       └── g.mu-logo-pencil                 ANIMATED: wiggle (pencil and the paw holding it)
    ├── path.mu-logo-lip               front lip of the burrow, drawn over the marmot
    └── g.mu-logo-sparkle              ANIMATED: wink sparkle (yellow star and a small white star)
```

The clip and the lip together make the marmot disappear "into" the burrow. Keep the clip on the outer, static `<g>` and animate only the inner `g.mu-logo-marmot`. If the clip were on the moving group, it would move with it.

## Static state and reduced motion

With no animation (the static file, `[animated]=false`, or `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`), the marmot is fully visible with both eyes open. The base styles hide the wink and the sparkle:

```css
.mu-logo-sparkle { opacity: 0; }
.mu-logo-wink { opacity: 0; }
```

Every animation runs only under `.mu-logo--animated`. The `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` block sets `animation: none`, so the marmot falls back to the visible static state.

Inline the SVG in the component template. Headless Chrome under Playwright does not pass emulated `reducedMotion` through to SVG documents loaded with `<img>`. Inline SVG respects it, which was verified.

## Transform origins

| Selector | transform-box | transform-origin | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `.mu-logo-marmot` | `view-box` | `64px 100px` | bottom center of the burrow, so squash and stretch grow from the hole |
| `.mu-logo-eye` | `fill-box` | `50% 50%` | blinks around each eye's center |
| `.mu-logo-pencil` | `view-box` | `83px 80px` | the paw that holds the pencil |
| `.mu-logo-sparkle` | `fill-box` | `50% 50%` | spins and scales in place |

## Keyframes (one 5 s loop, all `infinite`)

| Keyframes | Applied to | Timing function | Story |
|---|---|---|---|
| `mu-logo-pop` | `.mu-logo-marmot` | `cubic-bezier(.45,0,.25,1)` | 0–6% hidden (`translateY(72px)`); 14% stretched leap; 19% squash on landing; 24% settle; 29–78% rest; 83% anticipation squash; 91–100% ducked |
| `mu-logo-blink` | `.mu-logo-eye-left` | `linear` | blinks at 33.5% and 69.5% |
| `mu-logo-eye-right` | `.mu-logo-eye-right` | `linear` | the same blinks; closes at 46%, hidden 46.1–61.9% for the wink, reopens at 64% |
| `mu-logo-wink` | `.mu-logo-wink` | `step-end` | visible from 46% to 62% |
| `mu-logo-sparkle` | `.mu-logo-sparkle` | `ease-out` | pops in at 46–51% (scale 1.2, rotated 45°), fades out by 62% |
| `mu-logo-wiggle` | `.mu-logo-pencil` | `ease-in-out` | wiggles -12°, +10°, -8°, +5° between 44% and 64% |

In seconds: the marmot pops up at about 0.3–1.2 s, blinks at 1.7 s, winks with the sparkle and wiggles the pencil at 2.2–3.2 s, blinks again at 3.5 s, then squashes down from 3.9 s (deepest at 4.15 s) and is gone by 4.55 s. The burrow stays empty until the loop restarts.

## Porting to Angular (`<mu-logo [animated]>`)

1. Copy the SVG markup into `mu-logo.component.html` without the `<style>` element, and move the CSS into `mu-logo.component.scss`. Emulated view encapsulation works: the selectors only use classes from the template, and `@keyframes` names are not scoped, which is why they carry the `mu-logo-` prefix.
2. Bind the modifier class on the root `<svg>`: `[class.mu-logo--animated]="animated()"`, with `animated = input(false)`.
3. Make the ids unique per instance. The gradient and clip ids (`mu-logo-sky`, `mu-logo-meadow`, `mu-logo-fur`, `mu-logo-tile-clip`, `mu-logo-burrow-clip`) are document-global. Append a per-instance suffix, for example from a static counter, and bind it: `[attr.id]="'mu-logo-fur-' + uid"`, `[attr.fill]="'url(#mu-logo-fur-' + uid + ')'"`, `[attr.clip-path]="'url(#mu-logo-burrow-clip-' + uid + ')'"`.
4. Keep `role="img"` and `aria-label` (use `BRAND_NAME`), or set `aria-hidden="true"` when the logo sits next to visible brand text.
5. Optionally pause the animation when the logo is off screen (an IntersectionObserver toggling the modifier class) to save battery on tablets.
